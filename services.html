COMMON QUESTIONS

Quick answers to things people ask before starting a project.

How long does a typical IoT prototype take?

Most basic prototypes take 1–3 weeks from brief to delivery, depending on complexity and component availability. Simple sensor nodes can be done in days; multi-sensor automation systems with a dashboard typically take 2–4 weeks. You'll get a timeline in the proposal.

Do you ship hardware internationally from Tanzania?

Yes, for finished prototypes I can arrange shipping via DHL or similar carriers. For 3D enclosure designs I can deliver digital files (STL/STEP/F3D) instantly via email, so you can print locally. Remote IoT dashboard projects require no shipping at all.

What's the payment process?

I work with a 50% upfront deposit to start, and the remaining 50% on delivery. Payments can be made via M-Pesa, bank transfer, or crypto. For consultation and training sessions, full payment is required upfront. I'll confirm available options in the proposal.
